Make your own game

pinyol is designed to be used with a coding agent such as Codex or Claude Code. The agent reads the repository's instructions, asks you at most four questions (graphics, logo, universe and where it will live), decides the rest, follows the recipe for the look you ask for and tests the game before handing it over.

  1. Get the engine

    On a Mac with Homebrew, or on Linux.

    git clone https://github.com/ipaud/pinyol.git
cd pinyol

  2. Ask your agent

    Open the folder in your agent and tell it what you want, in your own language.

    Make me a platformer with a Game Boy look: a mouse looking for cheese in a library.

  3. Play it

    On the computer, or send it to the console over USB.

    ./build/pinyol_sim --game games/raton
No coding? The web editor does the same with drawings and blocks, in the browser.

What the engine brings

All of this works the same on the console, the computer and in the browser, and your agent knows it because it is in the repository's instructions.

  • Picture

    A virtual console like the Game Boy Advance: 240×160 with four layers, 128 sprites, palettes, transparency and per-line effects, and on top, text and panels at 480×320. Also PlayStation-style textured 3D faces, lights, particles and cut-out animation.

  • Worlds

    Mode 7, first person in the style of Wolfenstein 3D, voxel landscapes, grids with field of view and pathfinding, generated dungeons, swarms of hundreds of bullets and levels drawn in Tiled or LDtk.

  • Sound

    Sound effects made from recipes or from numbers, four synth voices like the Game Boy Advance's, instruments, PlayStation reverbs, layered music that changes on the bar line, and .mod, .xm and .s3m modules.

  • The studio

    A step sequencer inside the audio: tracks of sounds and an acid bass like a TB-303's, with echo, reverb and Lo-fi GBA. Every step sounds on its exact sample, and a new pattern comes in right on the next bar.

  • The console

    Touch screen with a stylus and several fingers, microphone, the LED and the battery, video from the card and games between nearby consoles, no router. The menu opens over any game, with settings, favourites and achievements.

  • Text and quality

    textbox fits text into its box, textw measures it before drawing it and the box library makes frames as big as their text. The simulator checks in Spanish, Catalan and English that nothing is cut, that nothing flashes dangerously and that pictures are fast on the console, and bots play every demo to the end.

  • For coding

    Lua, with more than thirty libraries built in: physics, particles, stories, camera, scenes, artificial intelligence, generated levels, Celeste's movement and the adventure kit. Games save without freezing, a script tests every game by itself and a profiler says where the time goes.

The editor

Draw your characters pixel by pixel, paint the maps, set the rules with blocks and play straight away. Start from scratch or from a template such as Abella or LÍNEA 0.

  • Console look Game Boy, Game Boy Advance, PICO-8, ZX Spectrum or 16-bit, with its colours and font
  • Drawings that move pixel by pixel, from the front, the side and the back, with animations to walk, take a hit or do up to six actions
  • Rules with blocks lives, countdowns, dialogue, turning towards someone, saved high scores and blinds between levels
  • Sound effects from recipes, music composed on a grid, or a .mod
  • To the console over the USB cable, or as a .zip for the microSD card
  • In three languages castellano, català and English

The ladder: five requests

From simplest to hardest. A clean clone of the repository, one request at a time and no questions answered: Codex read AGENTS.md, built the engine, followed the recipe for the look and wrote a script that plays its game, iterating until it passed. Each game was then measured on the console, running that script. Codex 0.155 with gpt-6-sol, medium reasoning. (The requests were written in Spanish and are translated here.)

Send it to the Library

When your game is ready, send it with the form: a robot opens it and plays it for ten seconds, and once we have reviewed it, it can be played on the site and downloaded for the console.

Submit your game (opens in a new tab)The Library

Want to send a new version of a game that is already in the Library? Send the form again, from the same GitHub account and with the same ID (the id in your game.json): once approved, it replaces the old one, and anyone already playing keeps their saved games. The game's page can take a little while to show the changes.

Is something of yours here without your permission, or something that shouldn't be here? Ask us to take it down (opens in a new tab).
